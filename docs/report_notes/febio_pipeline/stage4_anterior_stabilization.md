# FEBio Stage 4 Anterior Stabilization

## What stage 4 is really about

Stage 4 should currently be understood as:

- nipple or anterior-tip stabilization first
- asymmetry second

The main visible realism problem is not simply that the model is still too symmetric.
The bigger problem is that the nipple or anterior region can still look clipped, flattened, or merged too abruptly into the breast envelope.

## Main reasoning

The nipple-areolar complex should be treated as a distinct anterior region, not just as the front of a generic glandular ellipse.
That makes it more defensible to stabilize the anterior contour before introducing stronger profile or whole-breast asymmetry.

## Best stage ordering

1. stage 4: stabilize the nipple, anterior tip, and subareolar relation
2. stage 5: introduce asymmetry
3. later: add richer glandular or lobular realism on top of a stable front geometry

## Practical modelling direction

- preserve a small explicit anterior projection
- keep the glandular bulk mainly subareolar rather than turning the nipple into a blunt gland extension
- reintroduce broader profile variation only after the front silhouette is stable

## Practical conclusion

Stage 4 should be framed as a profile-realism and anterior-stability stage, not yet as full three-dimensional asymmetry.

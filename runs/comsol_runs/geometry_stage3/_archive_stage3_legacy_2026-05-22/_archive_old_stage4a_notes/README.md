# Stage 4A: Alternative Gland Representation

This stage sits between richer explicit gland geometry and later internal-support stages.

Use this folder for:

- lighter gland-representation experiments
- gland-fraction or gland-density field approaches
- interpolated or analytic gland maps
- comparisons between explicit lobules and smoother internal heterogeneity models

Goal:

- decide whether the explicit multi-ellipsoid gland approach remains the best COMSOL path
- or whether a field-based gland model gives a better realism-versus-cost balance before adding ligaments and tumor cases

Recommended order:

1. build-only visualization of the alternative gland representation
2. side-by-side comparison against the Stage 4 explicit gland branch
3. only then promote the chosen gland strategy into Stage 5

# Geometry Stage 2

This folder now represents the **material-calibration stage** of the FEBio improvement path.

Although stage 2 started as a geometry/support exploration step, the chest-wall preview branch was not kept as a report-quality result. The stable and useful outcome of this stage is instead a calibrated material ladder on a fixed stage-2 reference geometry.

## What stage 2 ended up being

Stage 2 should now be interpreted as:

- a controlled material-calibration stage
- on top of the successful mild elliptic geometry family
- before moving into a later stage-3 support/anatomy refinement step

It is **not** currently a successful chest-wall-refinement stage.

## Why the chest-wall preview branch was not kept

During the stage-2 exploration, several lightweight posterior/support previews were tested:

- `stage2_reference_mesh_preview`
- `stage2_upperpole_chest_preview`
- `stage2_curved_chest_preview`
- `stage2_curved_chest_preview_strong`
- `stage2_curved_chest_refinedmesh`

These were useful for debugging, but they were not kept as the stable report line because:

- the visible posterior curvature was not convincingly literature-aligned
- the simple curved-interface approach did not cleanly represent the torso/pectoralis support concept used in Chen-like multi-component models
- the branch temporarily destabilized the volume separation logic and therefore was not a good foundation for calibrated reporting

The stable stage-2 line therefore returns to the cleaner reference geometry and uses it for material calibration.

Purpose:

- soft stage-2 baseline

- acts as the low-stiffness anchor for comparison

- older softer material baseline

### `stage2_reference_intermediate_materials`

Purpose:

- review-guided intermediate calibration case
- sits between the old soft stage-2 setup and the full Chen-inspired material probe
- currently the best balanced stage-2 candidate

Important settings:

- `density = 125`
- `order = 1`

- adipose and glandular still clearly stiffer than the older soft baseline

### `stage2_reference_chen_materials`

Purpose:

- explicit Chen-inspired material probe
- upper bound on the current stiffness ladder
- useful as a literature-directed calibration endpoint, not automatically as the preferred final baseline

Important settings:

- `density = 125`
- `order = 1`
- explicit Chen-inspired material blocks for skin, adipose, and glandular tissue

## Interpretation

### Soft baseline

- displacement is probably too large
- stress is lower
- `J` is weaker

### Full Chen-inspired probe

- displacement is much more constrained
- `J` is improved
- stress becomes very high for the current simplified model

### Intermediate case

- displacement is substantially reduced relative to the soft baseline
- stress remains high, but clearly below the full Chen probe
- `J` remains much healthier than the soft baseline

This makes `stage2_reference_intermediate_materials` the most reasonable stage-2 outcome at the moment.

## Important stress caveat

The current stress values must be interpreted carefully.

In Chen-style multi-component literature, stress is distributed across:

- glandular tissue
- pectoralis major
- adipose tissue
- Cooper's ligaments

with ligaments typically taking the largest stress burden.

The current FEBio model in this stage does **not** yet include:

- an explicit pectoralis-muscle domain
- explicit Cooper's ligaments
- a fully literature-style torso support interface

So the current peak `vm_max` values are still being generated by a structurally simplified model. That means:

- the soft baseline is probably too compliant
- the full Chen-inspired case is probably too stress-concentrated
- the intermediate case is a better calibration compromise, but still not the final literature-perfect answer

## Stage-2 conclusion

Stage 2 is complete enough to close as a **material-calibration stage**.

Recommended stage-2 take-away:

- keep `stage2_reference_intermediate_materials` as the preferred stage-2 calibrated baseline
- keep `stage2_reference_refinedmesh` as the soft comparison anchor
- keep `stage2_reference_chen_materials` as the stiff literature-directed probe

## Suggested stage-3 scope

Stage 3 should now focus on support/anatomy rather than another big material jump.

Recommended direction:

- posterior/torso support refinement
- pectoralis-lite support concept
- mesh refinement and support-geometry cleanup
- only then revisit whether stress levels move closer to literature expectations
